# Releases

## 1.1.1

Allow each slide to provide separate localized long-form notes while keeping
concise on-slide summaries and captions unchanged.

## 1.1.0

Add a multilingual thank-you layout with a large stored QR image and readable
permanent presentation link. QR identities and redirect destinations stay in
individual deck configuration, outside the generic kit.

## 1.0.6

A brief liquid droplet travels between the circular counters when advancing or
reversing slides in Present. Rapid navigation cancels the previous motion;
reduced-motion settings suppress it.

## 1.0.5

Style both matching circular counters in dark blue with white Open Runde numbers.

## 1.0.4

Match the current and total slide badges at 46px with 24px rounded numerals.

## 1.0.3

Use bundled Open Runde Semibold for the circular slide numbers and reserve space
below the slide for the larger badges. Other typography is unchanged.

## 1.0.2

Place the larger current-slide number in a circle on the left of the progress
bar, with the total slide count in its own circle at the far right.

## 1.0.1

Synchronize reading-view position after leaving Present mode, and allow arrow
navigation after focus returns to the Present button.

## 1.0.0

Initial public component kit: configurable languages, Present/Exit, touch
navigation, glass captions, summaries, progress and optional live following.
Explicit version updates preserve deck content and custom themes.
